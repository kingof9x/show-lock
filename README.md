# Show Lock

A lock screen for Windows that doesn't blank your other screens.

Windows' own lock (Win+L) switches every display to the lock screen, which stops a
show or presentation on a projector. Show Lock locks the laptop's own screen instead
and leaves the other displays playing, untouched and unusable until you unlock.

- **Lock** with a shortcut (Ctrl+Alt+L by default) or from the tray icon.
- **Main screen**: a blurred picture of what was there. Any key or mouse movement
  shows a small password box.
- **Unlock** with your Show Lock password (separate from your Windows password) or
  your fingerprint (Windows Hello).
- **Other screens** keep showing what they were showing, but clicks and touches there
  are ignored.
